About Gmail scanning

Connecting Gmail is optional — the app works fully without it. If you do connect it, SubOrganizer asks for read-only access and uses it for one thing: recognising subscriptions from receipts, welcome emails and cancellation notices.

It reads those emails on your device. Nothing is uploaded, and the contents of your email are never stored. Only the subscription details you review and approve are saved — a name, an amount, a renewal date. It can never send, delete or change your mail, and you can disconnect at any time.

SubOrganizer's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
