Delete your account

SubOrganizer · Last updated 29 July 2026

This page explains how to delete your SubOrganizer account and everything stored with it.

How to request deletion

  1. Email taskteamprosupport@gmail.com from the address you signed up with. Sending from that address is how we confirm the account is yours.
  2. Use the subject line Delete my account.
  3. You do not need to give a reason, and we will not ask for one.

We action requests within 30 days, and in practice within a few days. We email you to confirm once it is done.

What gets deleted

DataWhat happens
Your account and sign-in detailsDeleted
Name and email addressDeleted
Every subscription you added, with amounts, cycles and notesDeleted
Reminder settingsDeleted
Your Gmail connection, if you used Gmail scanningRevoked and deleted

Nothing is kept. We hold no backups of your subscription data beyond routine database snapshots, which roll off within 30 days. After that no copy of your account remains.

Deleting some data without deleting your account

You do not have to close your account to remove data:

  • A single subscription — open it and tap Delete. It is removed immediately.
  • Your Gmail connection — Scan Gmail screen, then Disconnect. The grant is revoked with Google and the stored token is deleted.

SubOrganizer never uploads or stores the contents of your emails. Gmail scanning reads billing mail on your device, and only the subscription details you approve are saved. See the privacy policy for the full picture.
